Achieving highest confidence on the BlueRock's virtualization stack.

Verifying the BlueRock ULTRA™ Virtualization Stack

At BlueRock Security, we are on a mission to secure systems from the ground up. Formal verification is a crucial part of this goal because it is the only way to provide mathematical guarantees about a system. We apply decades of research on modular reasoning principles for highly concurrent software, in order to prove that our virtualization stack behaves as intended in all possible situations.

To date, we have specified and verified major parts of complex, concurrent C++ code across the BlueRock ULTRA™ stack, from the NOVA microhypervisor to user-mode applications, including a console multiplexer (UMX), a Virtual Machine Monitor (VMM), and a virtual Ethernet switch. These verifications have been collaborative efforts between systems engineers with no background in formal methods and formal methods engineers, and the experiences have been beneficial on both sides. Our verification efforts have found and fixed many subtle bugs, ranging from shallow undefined behavior to race conditions and semantic bugs across the entire stack.
