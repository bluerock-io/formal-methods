---
question: Why use separation logic?
---

[Separation logic](https://cacm.acm.org/research/separation-logic/) (SL) is a general-purpose logic for reasoning about independence, the principle that underlies all design principles. Rather than being tightly tied to any specific language, separation logic abstracts the actual state and focuses on the way the state can be divided and reasoned about independently. This abstraction has enabled separation logic to be applied in a wide range of reasoning domains including: a range of work on [weak memory](), [distributed systems](), and even [probability theory]().
Many of these applications were beyond the original motivation of separaiton logic, but, just like modern scientific theories, seperation logic has repeatedly demonstrated its firm grasp on the underlying problem.
